## 🎨 Color Palette

### Primary Colors

#### Cyan (Brand Start)
- **Hex:** `#22D3EE`
- **RGB:** `rgb(34, 211, 238)`
- **HSL:** `hsl(187, 85%, 53%)`
- **Usage:** Primary brand color, start of gradient, highlights
- **Represents:** Intelligence, technology, innovation

#### Orange (Brand End)
- **Hex:** `#F97316`
- **RGB:** `rgb(249, 115, 22)`
- **HSL:** `hsl(25, 95%, 53%)`
- **Usage:** Secondary brand color, end of gradient, accents
- **Represents:** Energy, warmth, approachability

#### Gradient (Signature)
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #22D3EE 0%, #F97316 100%);
```
- **Direction:** 135 degrees (diagonal)
- **Usage:** Logos, buttons, headers, key UI elements
- **Effect:** Creates sense of movement and innovation

### Supporting Colors

#### Dark Backgrounds
- **Primary Dark:** `#1E293B` (Slate-800)
- **Secondary Dark:** `#0F172A` (Slate-900)
- **Usage:** Backgrounds, cards, containers

#### Light Text
- **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Secondary Text:** `#E2E8F0` (Gray-200)
- **Tertiary Text:** `#94A3B8` (Gray-400)

### Accessibility
- Cyan on Dark Background: **AAA** rating
- Orange on Dark Background: **AAA** rating
- White on Cyan: **AA** rating
- White on Orange: **AAA** rating

## 🔤 Typography

### Primary Font
**Inter** (Sans-serif)
- **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold)
- **Usage:** All body text, UI elements, most headings
- **Characteristics:** Modern, clean, highly readable
- **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`

### Display Font
**Poppins** (Sans-serif) - Optional
- **Weights:** 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold)
- **Usage:** Large headlines, hero text, marketing materials
- **Characteristics:** Friendly, geometric, attention-grabbing

### Monospace Font
**Fira Code** (Monospace)
- **Weight:** 400 (Regular)
- **Usage:** Code snippets, technical documentation, data display
- **Fallback:** `'Courier New', monospace`

### Primary Logo
**Avatar Style:**
- Circular shape (width: 40-48px for standard, 64-80px for large)
- Gradient background (Cyan to Orange, 135deg)
- White letter "A" centered
- Bold, sans-serif typeface
- Clean, minimalist design

```html

  <span class="text-white font-bold text-xl">A</span>

```

### Logo Variations

#### 1. Avatar Only
- Circular "A" logo
- Usage: Chat bubbles, small icons, favicons

#### 2. Avatar + Wordmark
- Circular "A" + "Auralis AI" text
- Usage: Headers, business cards, presentations

#### 3. Full Lockup
- Circular "A" + "Auralis AI Solutions"
- Usage: Marketing materials, documentation

### Logo Clear Space
- Minimum clear space: 25% of logo height on all sides
- Never place logo on busy backgrounds
- Ensure sufficient contrast with background

### Minimum Sizes
- **Digital:** 32px × 32px (avatar)
- **Print:** 0.5 inch × 0.5 inch

### Logo Don'ts
❌ Don't stretch or distort the logo  
❌ Don't change the gradient colors  
❌ Don't add effects (shadows, glows, etc.)  
❌ Don't rotate the logo  
❌ Don't place on low-contrast backgrounds  
❌ Don't recreate or modify the logo

## 💬 Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Characteristics

#### Intelligent but Accessible
- Use clear, jargon-free language
- Explain complex concepts simply
- Show expertise without being condescending

#### Helpful and Proactive
- Anticipate user needs
- Offer relevant suggestions
- Guide users to solutions

#### Friendly but Professional
- Conversational tone
- Use contractions naturally
- Maintain professionalism
- Avoid overly casual language

#### Confident but Humble
- State capabilities clearly
- Admit limitations honestly
- Defer to human experts when appropriate

### Writing Style

#### Greetings
✅ "Hello! I'm Auralis, your AI assistant."  
✅ "Hi there! How can I help you today?"  
✅ "Welcome! I'm here to assist you."

❌ "Hey! What's up?"  
❌ "Yo! Need help?"

#### Responses
✅ "I can help you with that. Let me explain..."  
✅ "Here's what I found for you..."  
✅ "That's a great question. Here's the answer..."

❌ "Obviously, the answer is..."  
❌ "You should know that..."  
❌ "Let me Google that for you..."

#### Error Messages
✅ "I'm not sure I understood that. Could you rephrase?"  
✅ "I don't have that information, but I can connect you with someone who does."  
✅ "That's outside my current knowledge. Let me find a human expert for you."

❌ "Error 404: Response not found"  
❌ "I don't know"  
❌ "That's a stupid question"

### Personality Traits
- **Patient:** Never rushes the user
- **Encouraging:** Positive reinforcement
- **Clear:** No ambiguity in responses
- **Empathetic:** Acknowledges user emotions
- **Efficient:** Gets to the point quickly

## 🚫 Usage Restrictions

### What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't alter the gradient colors**
- The Cyan-Orange gradient is signature
- Don't use other color combinations
- Don't reverse the gradient direction

❌ **Don't use outdated branding**
- No generic "AI Assistant" labels
- Must use "Auralis AI" name
- No alternative logos

❌ **Don't mix with competitor branding**
- Don't place near ChatGPT, Copilot, etc. logos
- Maintain clear brand distinction

❌ **Don't use in misleading contexts**
- Don't imply capabilities we don't have
- Don't suggest human-level intelligence
- Be honest about AI limitations
